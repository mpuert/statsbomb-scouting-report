    What it says

    Passes + carries started in each zone during build-up

    Own goalAttacking →

    How to read this

    The pitch is split into a grid of zones (columns A–F from their own goal forward, rows 1–4 from their left touchline to their right). Each number counts the passes and carries the team started in that zone, only in possessions that began from regular play, a goal kick, a keeper restart or a kick-off (not from corners, free kicks or throw-ins). Dark zones are where they habitually move the ball out from the back. Heavy use of the defensive-third half-spaces means they build through the center-backs and holding midfielders rather than going long; if one flank is much darker than the other, their build-up leans to that side, and you can prepare your press for it.

    Pressure events started in each zone

    Own goalAttacking →

    How to read this

    Same zone grid. Each number counts the times this team closed down the opponent's ball carrier (a Pressure event) starting in that zone. Dark zones in the middle or attacking third mean they press aggressively to win the ball back high up the pitch (a high press). Dark zones mostly in their own defensive third mean they press less and wait in a mid or low block until the opponent is close to their goal.

    Delivery zone × technique

    How to read this

    Zone is where the delivery ended, seen from the corner-taker's side: Near Post (the post closest to the taker), Central, Far Post, or Short (played short and not into the box). Technique is how the ball was struck: inswinging (curls toward goal), outswinging (curls away) or straight. Use the filters to see each routine on the pitch.

    What it says

    A free kick's purpose depends on where it is taken. Defensive third: mostly about getting out of danger safely and restarting possession, usually short or backward. Middle third: moving the ball forward or switching play, with a mix of short passes and longer balls. Attacking third: in effect a set-piece delivery into the box, so these get the same zone and technique breakdown as corners. Click a third to show it on the pitch.

    Completion is the share of free kicks that reached a teammate.
